# Bulking vs. Cutting: Why Precision is the Difference Between Success and Plateau

Whether you are trying to pack on lean muscle during a bulk or shred down during a cut, your success is entirely dictated by mathematics. Working out provides the stimulus, but your kitchen provides the results. Yet, thousands of gym-goers hit plateaus every single day. Why? Because they aren't tracking precisely enough.

When you are on a cut, a miscalculation of just 200 calories a day can completely erase your caloric deficit. Eyeballing a scoop of peanut butter or guessing the weight of your rice might seem harmless, but over a week, those small errors compound into a massive roadblock. 

To break a plateau, you need absolute precision. If you struggle with weighing every single ounce of food, leverage modern technology. AI food scanners can act as a secondary check against your own estimates, ensuring that your 50g of protein is actually 50g. Stop leaving your hard work in the gym up to guesswork in the kitchen.
